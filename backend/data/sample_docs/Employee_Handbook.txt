ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (EXCERPTS)
Effective: January 2025 | Owner: People Operations
(Fictional sample document for hackathon demo purposes.)

Section 1.1 Welcome and Values
Our values are: customers first, ownership, transparency, and respect. This handbook is a
guide, not an employment contract.

Section 2.1 Onboarding
New joiners complete onboarding within their first 2 weeks, including HR orientation, IT
setup, and mandatory security training. A buddy is assigned for the first 30 days.

Section 2.2 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Flexible start between
8:30 AM and 10:30 AM is allowed with manager approval.

Section 3.1 Code of Conduct
Treat colleagues with respect. Harassment of any kind is prohibited and may lead to
termination. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR in writing.

Section 3.2 Dress Code
Smart casuals on working days; business formals for client visits.

Section 6.1 Performance Reviews
Performance is reviewed twice a year (June and December). Ratings determine annual
increments and bonuses.
