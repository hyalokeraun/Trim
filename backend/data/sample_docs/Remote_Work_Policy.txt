ACME CORPORATION — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Effective: April 2025 | Owner: People Operations | Version 1.8
(Fictional sample document for hackathon demo purposes.)

Section 1.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed 6 months and are rated "Meets Expectations" or above may
request hybrid or remote work with manager approval. Customer-facing support roles require
minimum 3 days in office per week.

Section 2.1 Hybrid Schedule
Hybrid employees work at least 2 days per week from the office. Teams fix common anchor
days. Yes — employees may work from home on Fridays if Friday is not their team's anchor
day and sprint commitments are met.

Section 2.2 Remote Work Expectations
Remote employees must be reachable on chat during working hours, keep calendars updated,
and attend all video meetings with cameras on unless exempted. Work from a location with
reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps).

Section 3.1 Equipment
The company provides a laptop, headset, and a one-time home-office allowance of
Rs. 15,000 against bills.

Section 4.1 Security While Remote
Remote work must follow the IT Security Policy: VPN with MFA is mandatory, and company
data must stay on company-managed devices.
