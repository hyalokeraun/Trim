ACME CORPORATION — HR LEAVE POLICY
Effective: January 2025 | Owner: People Operations | Version 3.1
(This is a fictional sample document created for hackathon demo purposes.)

Section 1.1 Employment Types
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Corporation. Part-time employees
accrue leave on a pro-rata basis. Contractors are not covered by this policy.

Section 4.1 Annual Leave (Earned Leave)
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of annual earned leave per calendar year. Leave accrues
at 1.5 days per month. Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the
next year. Carry-forward beyond 10 days lapses.

Section 4.2 Casual Leave
Employees get 12 casual leaves per year. Casual leave is intended for short, unplanned
absences such as minor illness, family needs, or personal errands. A maximum of 3 casual
leaves may be taken consecutively. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses at
the end of each calendar year. No prior approval is needed for 1 day, but inform your
manager over email or chat before 10 AM.

Section 4.3 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per year. Sick leave beyond 2 consecutive days
requires a medical certificate. Unused sick leave lapses yearly and cannot be encashed.

Section 4.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid, as per applicable law. Paternity leave is 15 days paid,
to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

Section 5.1 Leave Application Process
Apply for planned leave at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Casual leave for
one day can be applied retroactively within 2 working days. All leave must be approved by
the reporting manager.

Section 5.2 Leave Encashment
Unused annual leave up to 10 days may be encashed at the time of separation at the basic
daily rate. Casual and sick leave cannot be encashed.
