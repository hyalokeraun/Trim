ACME CORPORATION — IT SECURITY POLICY
Effective: March 2025 | Owner: Information Security | Version 2.4
(Fictional sample document for hackathon demo purposes.)

Section 1.1 Purpose
This policy defines minimum security requirements for all employees, contractors, and
systems handling company data.

Section 2.1 Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include uppercase, lowercase, a number,
and a special character. Passwords expire every 90 days. Reuse of the last 5 passwords is
prohibited. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, and code
repositories. Never share passwords over email or chat.

Section 2.2 Device Security
All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and auto-lock after 5 minutes of
inactivity. USB storage devices from outside the company are blocked by default; request
an exception from IT Security for approved drives.

Section 3.1 VPN and Remote Access
Employees working remotely must connect through the company VPN with MFA. Split tunneling
is disabled. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with VPN active.

Section 3.2 Data Handling
Customer data is classified as Confidential. Do not upload Confidential data to personal
cloud storage or public AI tools. Report suspected phishing within 1 hour to
security@acme.example. Suspected data breaches must be reported within 24 hours.

Section 4.1 Incident Reporting
Contact the IT helpdesk for malware alerts. For lost or stolen devices, notify IT Security
immediately so the device can be remotely wiped.
