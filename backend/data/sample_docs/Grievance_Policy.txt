ACME CORPORATION — GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL POLICY (EXCERPT)
Effective: January 2025 | Owner: People Operations
(Fictional sample document for hackathon demo purposes.)

Section 1.1 Scope
Any employee may raise grievances about workplace issues, excluding performance ratings
which follow the review appeal process.

Section 2.1 How to Raise a Grievance
Write to grievances@acme.example with facts and dates. The grievance officer acknowledges
within 3 working days. Resolution target is 15 working days.

Section 2.2 Escalation
If unresolved in 15 working days, escalate to the HR head, then to the ethics committee.
No retaliation is permitted against anyone raising a genuine grievance.
