ACME CORPORATION — TRAVEL & REIMBURSEMENT FAQ
Last updated: February 2025 | Owner: Finance
(Fictional sample document for hackathon demo purposes.)

Section 1.1 What expenses can I claim?
Employees can claim pre-approved business travel (flights, trains, hotels, local transport
for client visits) and client entertainment up to Rs. 2,500 per occasion with manager approval.

Section 1.2 Daily Allowance (Per Diem)
Domestic travel per diem is Rs. 1,800 per day covering meals and incidentals. International
per diem follows the country matrix published on the intranet.

Section 2.1 How do I submit a claim?
Submit claims in the expense portal within 15 days of travel with GST invoices. Claims above
Rs. 10,000 need director approval. Reimbursement is processed within 10 working days of approval.

Section 2.2 Flight and Hotel Limits
Domestic flights: economy class only. International flights over 6 hours: premium economy
with VP approval. Hotel caps: Rs. 6,000/night (Tier-1 cities), Rs. 4,000/night (other cities).

Section 3.1 Non-reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and mini-bar charges are not reimbursable.
